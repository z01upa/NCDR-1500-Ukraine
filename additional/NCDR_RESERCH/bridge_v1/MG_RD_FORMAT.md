# MG → RD bridge (Phase 1)

The prior scan's exact definition is `struct.pack(">HH", s65, s66)`, i.e. the four bytes of MG fields `s65` and `s66` in big-endian order. `s63/s64` are excluded because they are local endpoints.

`mg_rd_bridge.py` creates a resumable SQLite corpus with MG rows, signature metadata, RD hits, ±128-byte contexts, matched controls, and candidate offset summaries. The primary pass intentionally starts with the confirmed `suffix4_BE` key and its LE control; wider subsets are generated by the same `reps()` hook after the primary record structure is localized.

No RD record grammar is confirmed yet. The large hit volume means raw substring presence alone is insufficient; the next required filter is hit clustering against controls plus record/block boundaries.
