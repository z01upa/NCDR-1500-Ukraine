## Interpretation

`E & 0x3fff` is the strongest tested pointer model. The upper bits are stable grouping flags, but their semantic names are unresolved. F is a list/object count. A/B/C/D are ordered pair-like fields with weak global geographic correlation; the inequality pattern supports an index/range interpretation more than a confirmed bounding box. The exact type-0x00 element schema remains unresolved.
