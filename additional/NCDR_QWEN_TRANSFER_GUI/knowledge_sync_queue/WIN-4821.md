## Limits

- Compression `0x01` lacks a trusted decoded logical-buffer oracle; road geometry
  also remains unresolved. Local inference cannot guarantee a complete decoder.
- The model loop is finite (up to 200 calls per launch) and stops when evidence
  requires an unavailable oracle or its unique safe probes are exhausted.
- First setup requires internet for Python/Ollama installation and model download.

  validation passes.
